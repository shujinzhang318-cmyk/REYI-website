About REYI

REYI specializes in retail display and product experience for home appliance and consumer electronics brands.

15 Years of Experience

For 15 years, we have evolved alongside the products and technologies we work with, developing display and experience solutions for different products, technologies and retail environments.

End-to-End Capabilities

Our capabilities range from brand showrooms and shop-in-shops to display fixtures, product demonstration units, lightboxes and illuminated signage, covering development, engineering, prototyping, manufacturing and project delivery.

Product Demonstration Units

One of REYI’s long-established strengths is the development and engineering of product demonstration units. We look beyond how a product is displayed to how it is understood — turning airflow, water flow, operating states, performance and technical principles into experiences that consumers can see, feel, understand and interact with.

Long-Term Brand Experience

REYI has long-term experience serving leading global appliance and consumer electronics brands, including Siemens, Bosch, Samsung, Electrolux, Whirlpool, Midea, COLMO, TCL, Panasonic, Toshiba, Sony, Xiaomi, Gree and Dreame, across China and international markets.

From a single product demonstration unit to a complete branded retail environment, we understand the product — and how to help consumers understand it.
