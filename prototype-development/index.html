Prototype Development

Clarify the solution and the review scope before committing to batch production.

Agree the review stages

  • Requirement review: confirm the product, setting, quantity and feature or experience to communicate.
  • Drawing and technical review: discuss dimensions, construction, integration and access requirements.
  • Prototype scope: identify the fixture or mechanism to sample and the details that need customer review.
  • Material and finish review: review the proposed surfaces, graphics, lighting and brand requirements.
  • Demonstration review: where relevant, discuss what the mechanism needs to show and how it will be used.
  • Customer review and approval: agree the details and changes to be confirmed before production.

Evidence for the discussion
