Start Your Project

An idea, a product or a display problem is enough to begin a discussion. Share what you have; the first step is to understand what the display needs to do.

What can you share?

  • Product information: category, model, dimensions or the feature you want to explain.
  • An idea, reference images or drawings, if available. Send these by email or discuss them through WhatsApp.
  • The target market and intended setting: store, showroom, launch or experience zone.
  • Approximate quantity and timing, if known.
  • The display or experience problem: what should customers see, understand, compare or try?
