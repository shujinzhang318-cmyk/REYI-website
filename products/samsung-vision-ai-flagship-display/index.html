Product View

One architectural island, two connected stories

The circular platform, continuous illuminated frame and suspended rings define the installation as one complete product rather than a collection of separate fixtures. Neo QLED faces the main approach while the reverse side supports SmartThings and connected-home interaction. Customers can move around the display, compare content and use the central console without losing the flagship visual identity.

How the Experience Is Built

Four decisions turn technology into a clear flagship experience

360-degree Presence

A circular platform and double-sided layout create strong visibility from multiple store approaches while keeping customer circulation open.

Two Experience Faces

Neo QLED presentation and SmartThings interaction occupy opposite sides, giving each story a clear role within one installation.

Integrated Interaction

The central console brings product information and guided demonstration into comfortable reach without interrupting the main screen view.

Architectural Light

Continuous frame lighting and layered overhead rings extend the Vision AI identity beyond the screen and make the display legible at distance.

Material & Lighting Details

The flagship character comes from three controlled details

The reverse face turns SmartThings into a complete connected-home demonstration while remaining part of the same 360-degree flagship island.

The rounded frame, inset light line and layered overhead rings create a continuous architectural gesture with precise depth and junctions.

The platform edge, lower light channel and console base align as one durable material system with controlled floor contact and cable access.
