Product View

Many devices, organized as one clear experience

The table uses one continuous black-and-white architectural form to hold a wide product range without visual noise. Phones, tablets and wearables sit within defined dark display zones, making categories easier to scan while leaving a clean white perimeter for comfortable touch, comparison and conversation. The long footprint supports several customers at the same time without breaking the store's visual rhythm.

How the Experience Is Built

Four decisions keep a large assortment easy to explore

Clear Product Zones

Dark inset plates group phones, tablets and wearables into readable families while the white surface keeps the full table visually open.

Comfortable Access

Rounded corners, generous reach distances and multiple experience positions let customers approach, compare and stay without blocking one another.

Controlled Connections

Security stands, charging points and cable entries are coordinated with each device position so the working hardware remains orderly and serviceable.

One Store Language

The white top, continuous black band and flush cabinet panels connect the table to Samsung's broader retail environment without competing with the devices.

Material & Lighting Details

The experience depends on three controlled details

The table creates enough width, reach and device spacing for customers to compare products comfortably and remain engaged at the display.

The continuous radius and aligned black-white layers soften a large fixture while keeping the cabinet precise and visually quiet.

Device plates, stand bases and cable entries create a repeatable module that can change with each product generation.
