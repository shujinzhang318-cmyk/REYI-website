Product View

One continuous wall turns a large appliance range into two understandable stories

The complete product is the full two-zone experience wall. The left zone combines stacked appliances, an illuminated garment-care cabinet and supporting display bays to create a lifestyle-led demonstration area. The right zone arranges the broader wash-and-dry range in a disciplined two-row matrix, making product families easier to compare. Shared gold-toned frames, soft perimeter lighting and illuminated category bars hold both zones together as one premium brand environment.

How the Experience Is Built

Four decisions keep a dense laundry category clear, premium and easy to explore

Two-zone Category Architecture

Distinct left and right zones separate demonstration-led storytelling from full-range comparison while retaining one consistent visual language.

Illuminated Demonstration Focus

The lit garment-care cabinet gives the wall a human-scale focal point and helps shoppers connect appliances with clothing care and everyday use.

Modular Appliance Matrix

Precisely aligned bays support stacked and side-by-side formats, allowing multiple models and finishes to be presented in a compact, comparable system.

Warm Architectural Identity

Gold-toned rounded frames, warm-white surfaces, concealed lighting and an illuminated Midea logo elevate the wall beyond a conventional appliance lineup.

Material & Lighting Details

The experience becomes clear through three controlled details

The left zone combines stacked white appliances, an illuminated garment-care cabinet and dark side-by-side units, creating contrast without breaking the shared modular grid.

The two-row product matrix keeps a large model range visually ordered, with consistent openings, aligned control panels and restrained variation in appliance finishes.

The illuminated identity, softly glowing category bar, gold rounded frame and controlled cabinet joints give the wall a finished architectural character at close range.
