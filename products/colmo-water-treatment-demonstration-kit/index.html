Display design

A clear sequence for sample comparison

Three topic panels divide the kit into small presentation areas that a member of staff can introduce in turn.

Arrange samples in pairs

The vessels sit in pairs beneath the information panels, giving each comparison a defined place. The layout suits a guided discussion at a water-treatment retail counter or showroom.

Keep supporting materials close

Colour-reference strips and recessed tray positions provide space for the materials used during the presentation. Keeping these beside the vessels helps staff move through the sample sequence without spreading items across the counter.
