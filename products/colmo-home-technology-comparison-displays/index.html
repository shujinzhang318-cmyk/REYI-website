COLMO Home Device Display Panel

A sloped panel groups home devices and labelled mounting positions for a countertop retail or showroom presentation.

Organise the device positions

A socket and two round positions sit across the upper part of the board, with labels beneath them. The angled panel brings the devices and their identification into one viewing area.

Present devices within a compact footprint

The raised board and supporting base suit a counter where visitors can view the devices together. The separate application illustration shows a device arrangement for discussing the presentation format.

Display format

Shirt Sample Comparison Display

A covered display places two shirt samples side by side for a guided comparison on a retail or showroom counter.

Keep the garments side by side

The shirts occupy separate positions within one case, letting visitors compare their visible appearance without handling the samples.

Keep the explanation beside the samples

Labels provide a place to identify each sample during the presentation. The enclosed format keeps the pair together as a compact tabletop reference.

Display format

Showerhead and Metal Dish Comparison Display

A covered countertop display brings paired showerheads and metal dishes together for a guided surface comparison.

Separate the two sample pairs

Showerheads and metal dishes form distinct comparison groups inside the case. Each pair can be discussed separately while remaining part of the same tabletop presentation.

Make the sample surfaces visible

The transparent enclosure keeps the showerhead faces and dish surfaces in view. This format suits a retail or showroom counter where staff explain visible differences between prepared samples.
