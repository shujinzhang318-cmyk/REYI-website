TCL Airflow Demonstration Display

Airflow is one of the key advantages of air-conditioning technology, yet customers cannot see it during the buying journey.

REYI developed this retail demonstration display to transform invisible air movement into a visible and intuitive customer experience. By combining a transparent structure with optical rotating elements, the display helps shoppers understand product performance beyond specifications and supports stronger in-store communication.

Product View

From invisible airflow to visible experience

Traditional retail displays can show the product, but they cannot show how air actually moves. This TCL demonstration solution introduces a visible interaction point that helps customers understand airflow performance in a direct and memorable way. The structure stays visually light, integrates with the product body and turns a technical feature into an engaging retail experience.

How the Experience Is Built

Four decisions turn technical performance into customer understanding

Make Airflow Visible

The rotating optical element gives customers a clear visual cue of air movement, turning an invisible feature into an understandable experience.

Keep the Structure Light

The transparent band allows the demonstration device to remain readable without visually blocking the product itself.

Integrate With the Product

The demonstration element sits directly on the air outlet zone, creating a strong relationship between product function and customer perception.

Support Rollout

The concept is designed not just as a one-off sample but as a repeatable solution that can support wider retail deployment.

Material & Experience Details

The display experience depends on three controlled details

The installed detail makes the demonstration read as part of the product experience, helping customers connect visible motion with real airflow performance.

The transparent optical structure keeps the demonstration lightweight while focusing attention on movement, color and visible interaction.

The clean product interface reinforces that this is a real retail solution built around an actual branded product, not a conceptual mock-up.
