Product View

Two demo towers connect product installation with technical explanation

The complete product is the paired display system, not either blue panel alone. The narrower ALL EASY Pro tower explains maintenance and X-BLOCK construction, while the wider Cassette tower gives the four-way cassette unit a larger illuminated field and a dedicated Prime Guard information deck. In the installed state, both indoor units sit inside their light outlines without covering the brand, claim or technical content, so customers can compare formats and understand the story in one view.

How the Experience Is Built

Four decisions keep complex HVAC information clear and comparable

Paired Product Architecture

Two freestanding towers share one visual language while retaining the correct size, mounting field and information hierarchy for each indoor-unit format.

Appliance-ready Light Frames

Integrated outlines identify the mounting position when empty and remain visible around the installed units, turning the product silhouette into a consistent visual anchor.

Layered Feature Communication

No.1 Inverter positioning sits at the top, product names define the middle, and maintenance, X-BLOCK and Prime Guard explanations move closer to customer reach.

Integrated Demonstration Decks

Angled illuminated decks present component diagrams and durability stories at a comfortable reading angle while also forming stable architectural bases.
