Confirm the production requirements

  • Quantity: total units, fixture variants and allocation between stores or destinations.
  • Repeatability: approved dimensions, materials, finishes, graphics and product integration details.
  • Packaging: handling needs and how components will be identified for each destination.
  • Destination: domestic or overseas market and the requirements to discuss for that location.
  • Installation context: site dimensions, access, assembly and connection requirements where relevant.
  • Timing: the required project schedule, to be reviewed against the confirmed scope.
