SAMSUNG MOBILE · 2024

Samsung Mobile Experience Store

A mobile retail environment built around accessible product tables, clear comparison zones and coordinated brand presentation for smartphones, wearables, accessories and connected devices.

Project Year: 2024Location: Guangzhou, ChinaProject Type: Mobile & Consumer-Electronics Experience Store

Store Type

Samsung mobile retail store · Tianhe CBD, Guangzhou

Product Category

Smartphones, wearables, accessories, TV, and connected ecosystem products

Display Scope

Storefront branding, central product tables, Galaxy wall, accessory displays, lighting, and signage

Project Value

A store journey where a cool black technology identity is softened by an inward-curved entrance and rounded-corner display tables, creating an open gesture that draws customers in from across the mall

An Open Storefront with Clear Brand Visibility

The storefront establishes the Samsung identity before a customer enters. Illuminated Samsung and Galaxy signage, hero digital content and an open view into the store make the brand and product focus easy to read from the surrounding retail environment.

The entrance is not only a visual statement. It creates a clear starting point for the customer journey, showing that the space is more than a phone counter and inviting visitors into a wider mobile and connected-device experience.

A Clear Journey Through the Samsung Ecosystem

Inside the store, central display tables create the main hands-on interaction area. Customers can move from smartphones to wearables, accessories, digital content and connected-product zones without losing the overall store logic.

Clear zoning helps customers compare devices while also understanding how individual products connect within a wider ecosystem. The store supports independent browsing, staff explanation and a more natural movement from product discovery to decision-making.

Display Tables Built for Trial, Comparison and Daily Use

A mobile display table needs to look clean while managing live devices, security holders, product labels, information cards, charging and data cables, repeated handling and daily cleaning. These details directly affect how comfortable customers feel when picking up a phone or comparing several models.

Practical organisation also supports store staff. Clear device positions, controlled wiring and accessible information help maintain order, simplify product explanation and keep the display usable during busy retail hours.

A Coordinated Fixture System for Consistent Retail Experience

Storefront signage, Galaxy brand walls, digital-screen structures, product tables, accessory panels and island fixtures need to work as one system. Consistent proportions, details and brand communication allow different zones to feel connected without making the store repetitive.

REYI supported the translation of the approved retail concept into coordinated, manufacturable display components. The project demonstrates how fixture development, engineering coordination and custom production can create a practical mobile-retail language that can be adapted to the confirmed store format and product mix.
