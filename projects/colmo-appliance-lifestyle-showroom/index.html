Appliances within everyday living settings

Dark display fixtures, integrated lighting and COLMO branding connect the different presentation areas. Appliances appear both in dedicated product zones and within furnished settings.

Create a recognisable focal point

A triangular illuminated feature stands beside appliance displays and branded counters. Its distinct shape gives the retail area a visual focus alongside the individual product presentations.

Kitchen, living and appliance zones

Kitchen cabinetry and an island present appliances in a domestic setting, giving staff a place to discuss how products sit within a kitchen. The furnished living area provides another setting for product conversations, while dedicated appliance walls support category browsing. This mix is a useful reference for showroom briefs that combine product selection with lifestyle presentation.

A dedicated laundry presentation

Two front-loading appliances sit beside a branded island divider. Keeping the machines together creates a focused laundry zone where staff can explain each product without moving between separate display areas.

Bring branding into the fixture

Illuminated lettering sits above the appliances, close to the product presentation. Together with the island and wall fixtures, it gives the laundry area its own identity within the wider showroom.
