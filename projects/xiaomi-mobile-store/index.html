A Complete Store Across Mobile and Smart Living

The store brings phones, wearables, accessories, smart devices and selected home appliances into one connected Xiaomi retail environment. Open experience tables, wall displays, digital screens and lifestyle product zones create a clear route through different product categories.

The result is a complete store where customers can move from personal devices to a broader smart-living ecosystem without losing the overall brand language.

An Open Entrance Makes the Store Easy to Read

The glass storefront, illuminated Xiaomi signage and visible experience tables allow customers to understand the store before entering. Product islands and digital screens remain visible from the mall aisle, creating a direct visual invitation into the space.

This open presentation supports both quick visits and longer product exploration.

Experience Tables Support Direct Product Comparison

Multiple rows of mobile-device tables create clear opportunities to handle, compare and explore products. Consistent spacing and repeated fixture details help customers focus on the devices rather than the display system itself.

The open layout also preserves sightlines toward accessories and other product categories around the store.

Clear Fixtures Keep Technology Approachable

Mobile phones and wearables are presented on clean, accessible tables with simple device supports and controlled product spacing. The fixtures give each device enough presence while maintaining the light and practical character associated with the Xiaomi retail environment.

This makes the technology feel available for everyday trial rather than separated behind a formal barrier.

Accessories Extend the Mobile Experience

Accessory walls and adjacent displays add depth to the mobile journey by placing related products within easy reach of the main experience tables. Clear organization helps customers understand how devices, wearables and accessories work as part of a wider product system.

Digital communication screens provide additional brand and product context without overcrowding the fixtures.

Smart-Living Zones Broaden the Product Story

Lifestyle and smart-device displays extend the store beyond personal electronics. Shelving and product islands introduce connected products in a format that remains consistent with the mobile experience areas.

These zones show how Xiaomi products can enter different parts of everyday life while remaining part of one retail journey.

Home Appliances Complete the Ecosystem

Kitchen appliances, refrigerators and other home products create a final transition from personal technology to the wider home environment. Dedicated wall displays and category groupings help these larger products remain organized within the same store.

Together, the mobile, smart-device and appliance zones present a more complete view of the Xiaomi ecosystem.

What This Project Demonstrates

  • A complete-store system can connect mobile devices, accessories, smart products and appliances within one brand environment.
  • An open storefront helps customers understand the experience before entering.
  • Repeated experience tables make mobile-device comparison direct and approachable.
  • Accessory and smart-living zones extend the journey beyond the main product tables.
  • Home-appliance displays complete the transition from personal technology to connected living.
