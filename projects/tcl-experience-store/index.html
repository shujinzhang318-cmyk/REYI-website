A Complete Store Built Around Home Entertainment

Rather than presenting televisions as isolated products, the store is organized as a complete home-entertainment journey. Large screens, sofa viewing areas, feature walls, a private-cinema setting and smart-living products are connected through one spatial language.

This allows different categories to remain distinct while still feeling part of the same TCL retail environment.

Living-Room Settings Make Large Screens Easier to Imagine

Sofa seating and living-room-style display zones give customers a familiar reference for judging scale, viewing distance and atmosphere. The products are not only seen on a wall; they are experienced in a setting closer to everyday use.

For large-format television retail, this kind of context can make product size and presence easier to understand.

Distinct Display Zones Clarify Different Viewing Experiences

The store uses separate presentation zones for different screen technologies and viewing scenarios. Feature walls, multi-screen arrangements and open circulation help customers move between products without losing the overall brand story.

Each zone has its own visual focus, while repeated lighting, signage and furniture details maintain continuity across the store.

Private Cinema Turns Product Scale into Experience

The private-cinema area creates a more immersive setting for a large-screen television. Lounge seating, controlled lighting and a star-effect ceiling encourage customers to pause and experience the screen as part of an entertainment moment rather than as a specification alone.

This gives the largest products a setting that matches their visual scale.

Extending the Store Beyond Television

The TCL smart-lock display broadens the retail story from home entertainment to connected living. Keeping it within the same store shows how different TCL product categories can share a common brand environment without competing for attention.

The result is a more complete view of how the brand may enter different parts of everyday home life.
